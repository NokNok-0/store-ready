ABOUT THE APP
Streaks is a habit tracker: add daily habits, check them off once a day, see each habit's current and best streak, and attach a photo to a habit. All data is stored on the device. The app has no server, no ads, no tracking and no in-app purchases.

DEMO ACCOUNT
Accounts are created and stored on the device, so there is no server-side account to share. Create the demo account on the review device:

1. Member (the only role)
   Username: reviewer@storeready.dev
   Password: any password of 8 or more characters
   On a fresh install, tap "Create an account" on the first screen, enter the email above and a password, and tap "Create account". After that, "Sign in" works with the same email and any 8+ character password.
   Sees: every feature. There are no other roles and no paid tier.

SIGN-IN AND TWO-FACTOR AUTHENTICATION
There is no two-factor authentication and no code is sent. After "Create account" or "Sign in" the Today screen opens.
"Continue with Apple" is on the same screens (iOS only) and signs in with the reviewer's Apple Account; it also needs no further step in the app.

HOW TO REACH EACH FEATURE
- Add a habit: Today > type a name in "New habit" > Add.
- Check off a habit for today: Today > tap the circle on the habit's row.
- Habit details (current streak, best streak, check-ins): Today > tap the habit's name.
- Add a photo to a habit: Today > tap the habit > Take photo (camera) or Choose photo (photo library). The camera and photo permissions are asked only here; if you decline, the app explains how to turn access on and everything else keeps working.
- Delete a habit: Today > tap the habit > Delete habit > Delete.
- Privacy policy and support: Today > Settings > Privacy policy / Help and support.
- Delete account: Today > Settings > Delete account > Delete account > confirm. This deletes the account, its habits and photos from the device and returns to the sign-in screen. To keep reviewing afterwards, create the demo account again as above.
