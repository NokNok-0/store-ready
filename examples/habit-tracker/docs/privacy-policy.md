# Streaks privacy policy

Effective date: 7 November 2026

Streaks is a habit tracker for iPhone, iPad and Android, published by an individual developer (GitHub: NokNok-0). This policy explains what the app does with your information. In short: everything you put into Streaks stays on your device. The app has no servers, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

## What Streaks stores, and where

Streaks stores the following on your device only, in the app's own storage:

- Your account profile: the email address you enter when you create an account, or the email Apple shares with the app if you use Sign in with Apple (this may be a private relay address, or nothing if you choose not to share it), the sign-in method you used, and the date the account was created. Streaks does not store your password.
- If you use Sign in with Apple: the user identifier Apple gives this app, so the app can recognise you the next time you sign in. Streaks does not store Apple tokens.
- Your habits: their names, the days you checked them off and the date you created them.
- Photos you add to a habit: a reference to the picture you took with the camera or chose from your photo library. The picture stays on your device.

None of this is sent to the developer or to anyone else. The developer cannot see, access or recover it.

## Permissions

- Camera: used only when you tap "Take photo" on a habit, to take a picture for that habit.
- Photo library: used only when you tap "Choose photo" on a habit, to pick a picture for that habit.

Both are optional. If you decline, the rest of the app keeps working. You can change these permissions at any time in your device settings.

## What Streaks does not do

- It does not collect data: nothing you enter or create in the app leaves your device.
- It does not use analytics, crash reporting, advertising or tracking SDKs.
- It does not sell or share your information with anyone.
- It does not use your information to train or call any AI service.

## Deleting your data

You can delete your account and all of its data inside the app: Settings > Delete account. This removes your profile, your habits, their check-in history and the photos you added to habits from the app's storage, and signs you out. Deleted data cannot be restored.

Uninstalling the app also removes everything it stored. More detail, including how to delete without opening the app: [Delete your Streaks account](delete-account.md).

## Children

Streaks is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

## Your rights

Because the developer holds no personal data about you, there is nothing for the developer to access, correct, export or delete on your behalf: all of your data is on your device and under your control. If you have a question about your data or this policy, open an issue at https://github.com/NokNok-0/store-ready/issues.

## Changes

If the app starts collecting or sharing any information, for example when a subscription or sync is added, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the effective date above will change.
