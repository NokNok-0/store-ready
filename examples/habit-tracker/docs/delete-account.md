# Delete your Streaks account

App: Streaks (Google Play and App Store). Developer: NokNok-0.

## Delete your account in the app

1. Open Streaks and sign in.
2. Tap Settings (top right of the Today screen).
3. Tap Delete account.
4. Read what will be deleted, tap Delete account, then confirm.

The app deletes your account and signs you out straight away.

## Delete without opening the app

Streaks keeps your account and all of its data only on your phone or tablet. The developer has no server and no copy of your data, so there is nothing to request from the developer.

To delete everything without signing in, uninstall Streaks:

- Android: press and hold the Streaks icon, tap App info, then Uninstall. Or open Settings > Apps > Streaks > Uninstall.
- iPhone and iPad: press and hold the Streaks icon, tap Remove App, then Delete App.

Uninstalling removes your account, habits, check-in history and the photos you added to habits. You do not need to reinstall the app to delete your data.

If you have a question about deletion, open an issue at https://github.com/NokNok-0/store-ready/issues.

## What is deleted

- Your account profile: email address, sign-in method, creation date, and the Apple user identifier if you used Sign in with Apple.
- Every habit, its check-in history, and the photos you added to habits.

## What is kept

Nothing. Streaks has no server, so no data is kept anywhere after deletion. Deletion happens immediately and cannot be undone.

If you used Sign in with Apple, you can also remove Streaks from the apps that use your Apple Account: open your Apple Account settings, find Sign in with Apple, choose Streaks and stop using your Apple Account with it.

See also the [Streaks privacy policy](privacy-policy.md).
